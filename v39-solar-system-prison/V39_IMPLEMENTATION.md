# V39 — なぜ人類は太陽系を永遠に出られないのか？

## Production intent
- 72 finely segmented narrative scenes.
- Script-driven animation: each beat has a unique `visual` key and explicit visual intent.
- Background groups are reused only for contiguous continuity beats. Background layers are intentionally static; only foreground objects animate during consecutive same-background scenes.
- No external footage, stock loops, or repeated generic card background.
- Original Remotion vector/typographic animation in 1920x1080 / 30fps.
- Measured 青山龍星 VOICEVOX timing; subtitles are generated from the active measured beat.
- Parallel segmented rendering and scene-complete 72-frame QA contact sheet.

## Visual grammar
The film moves through rooftop stargazing, imagined warp travel, mission control, heliosphere, heliopause crossing, Oort-cloud shell, AU distance ruler, Voyager timelines, Proxima scale, Parker Solar Probe, energy chamber, braking flyby, rocket-equation board, fuel spiral, propulsion lab, laser sail, interstellar-dust impacts, generation ship, closed ecosystem, population deck, council deck, exoplanet arrival, multi-year communication, branching civilizations, and final Earth lifeboat.

## Continuity rule
When consecutive scenes share `backgroundGroup`, the background component receives no frame/progress-dependent transforms. Motion is restricted to foreground probes, particles, gauges, labels, people, ships, shields, light pulses, and diagrams. This prevents visible background resets between adjacent scenes while preserving motion in the explanatory layer.

## Core thesis
宇宙は途方もなく広い。しかし「存在している世界」と「人類が実際に到達し、文明を運べる世界」は同じではない。恒星間航行の難しさは一個の巨大技術課題ではなく、距離・速度・エネルギー・燃料・衝突・寿命・生態系・遺伝・教育・政治が連鎖する問題である。
