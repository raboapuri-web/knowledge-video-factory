Production framing:
- “Strong responsibility” is not treated as inherently harmful.
- The film distinguishes responsibility from over-control, delegation from abandonment, and short-term task efficiency from long-term team capacity.
- “If I am absent, the team stops” is visualized as a single-point-of-failure organizational design problem, not a personality diagnosis.
