## Script accuracy notes
- 2026 WHR rankings measure 2023–2025 adult/resident population retrospective life evaluations, NOT 2026 newborn outcomes.
- UNICEF Report Card 20 issued May 2026, 44 countries covered in inequality work, 37 in 3-dimension child-well-being table. Distinguish from 2025 Report Card 19.
- UNICEF ranking is for observed children at specific ages and data years, not a prediction for newborns in 2026.
- The 77% vs 67% life satisfaction figures are reported descriptive figures for sampled children from different socioeconomic backgrounds, not causal effects nor universal rates.
- Costa Rica and Colombia ~37% refers to the report's child income poverty measure, NOT prevalence of hunger.
- Finland / Iceland / Denmark / Costa Rica rankings in WHR are the report's published results, not recommendations of which country an individual should choose.
- Family vignettes and future stories are explicitly fictional illustrations, not portraits of actual residents or predictions about cohorts.
- The philosophical ending ("人生とは、選べなかった始まりを、選び直していく時間である。") is the video's interpretative thesis, NOT an empirically derived universal outcome.
