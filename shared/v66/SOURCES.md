# V66 historical primary sources and academic bibliography
The first-person hoplite is an illustrative composite. The physical choreography is original animation, not a verified reconstruction of a particular historical individual.

Primary sources:
- Thucydides, History of the Peloponnesian War 5.70–74: Spartan flute marching; shield-side anxiety and rightward drift at Mantinea 418 BCE; outcome and pursuit. https://www.livius.org/sources/content/thucydides-historian/the-battle-of-mantinea-418-bce/
- Herodotus, Histories 7.211, 7.229–231: feigned flight at Thermopylae and competing accounts of Aristodemus's survival and subsequent exclusion. https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.01.0126%3Abook%3D7
- Xenophon, Memorabilia 3.1 (front and rear ranks), Perseus Greek and translation. https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.01.0208%3Abook%3D3
- Tyrtaeus, elegiac fragments 10 and 12, preserved poetic ideals. Poetry is not an individual soldier's memoir.

Research:
- Crowley, Jason (2012), The Psychology of the Athenian Hoplite: The Culture of Combat in Classical Athens. Cambridge University Press.
- Krentz, Peter (1985), The Nature of Hoplite Battle, Classical Antiquity 4(1):50–61.
- Krentz, Peter (1985), Casualties in Hoplite Battles, Greek Roman and Byzantine Studies 26(1):13–20. https://grbs.library.duke.edu/article/view/6071/5707
- Kagan, Donald & Viggiano, Gregory F., eds. (2013), Men of Bronze: Hoplite Warfare in Ancient Greece. Princeton University Press.
- Devereaux, Bret (2022), Total Generalship: Morale and Cohesion, interpretation of casualty accounting and pursuit. https://acoup.blog/2022/07/01/collections-total-generalship-commanding-pre-modern-armies-part-iiic-morale-and-cohesion/

Cautions: ancient source narratives are selective and subject to debate; overlapping shield choreography, the othismos pushing model, and actual casualty denominators are disputed. Five percent winner and fourteen percent loser refer to mean attested full-battle death estimates, not a mortality rate from turning one's back. Strategic withdrawal is not the same as individual flight under contact. The story depicts no verified combatant's inner monologue, and gives no guarantee that standing firm ensured survival. Keep violence non-graphic and educational.
