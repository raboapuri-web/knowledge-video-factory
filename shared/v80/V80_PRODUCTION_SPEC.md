# V80 Concentration — original visual production specification
Full original ~20–23 minute Japanese documentary. Source script contains 40 detailed phases with progressing answer only at the end. Approx 280–380 micro-scenes from sentence splits; >=140 consecutive-only, never elsewhere-reused background groups, max two consecutive beats with shared plate and zero camera pan/zoom on that plate. In same-group transitions hold fixed room, desk, windows and large furniture while adding/removing/moving foreground phone, hand, notification, book, browser tabs, task files, clock, study lab shapes, attention paths, classrooms, test stimuli and past-vs-present objects. Every beat has unique frame-specific visual identity and semantic overlay linked to words, not only a generic looping background. Distinct visual worlds: 23:42 bedroom and book, rumor goldfish aquarium, pedestrian traffic, cinema, 2004 office, modern office with 3 displays, desk notification, attention-residue task stacks, short-video carousel, old library, information abundance, media studio, research lab (task accuracy not universally falling), controlled phone internet blocking, train reader, school math class, 18th/20th-century study, childhood summer, busy adult evening, return to bedroom. Human faces and UI no text other than mandatory subtitles. Japanese VOICEVOX 青山龍星 + BGM at low volume; original 1920x1080, 30fps. No reuse of identical stock plates among separated phases. Maintain scientific caution: measured switching not cognitive ability, self-report not task performance, intervention not irreversible brain change.
