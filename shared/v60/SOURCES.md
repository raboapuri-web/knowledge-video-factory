Cautions: Temporal Motivation Theory is a model of motivation/procrastination, not a precise biological formula for every person. Hyperbolic discounting is an explanatory family of models, not evidence that everyone's motivation rises abruptly. The story's “9000 versus 10000” and anxiety-relief cycle are illustrative hypotheticals, not direct experimental observations. Do not assert that deadline pressure causes a specific dopamine spike or that anxiety relief necessarily reinforces procrastination for everyone. Severe/chronic stress can impair performance; the depiction is narrative, not medical advice.
