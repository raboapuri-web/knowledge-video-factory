# V113 Sources

- Organ, D. W. (1988). Organizational Citizenship Behavior: The Good Soldier Syndrome. Lexington Books.
- Bolino, M. C. et al. Research on citizenship pressure, citizenship fatigue, compulsory citizenship behavior and the costs of extra-role behavior.
- De Clercq, D. et al. Research examining citizenship pressure and citizenship fatigue.
- Demerouti, E., Bakker, A. B., Nachreiner, F., & Schaufeli, W. B. (2001). The Job Demands–Resources model of burnout. Journal of Applied Psychology.
- Bakker, A. B. & Demerouti, E. (2007). The Job Demands–Resources model: state of the art. Journal of Managerial Psychology.
- Levitt, B. & March, J. G. (1988). Organizational Learning. Annual Review of Sociology, 14, 319–340.
- Experimental and field literature on target ratcheting: current performance affecting future targets can induce effort withholding and strategic output suppression.
- Recent work on high-performance punishment: extra responsibilities without commensurate rewards/recognition can encourage knowledge hiding and defensive behavior.

## Production interpretation
The film must not claim that every high performer is punished or that all OCB is harmful. The central mechanism is conditional: when reliable performance repeatedly produces extra demands without matching resources, compensation, authority, recovery time, or task subtraction, the incentive structure can make visible competence personally costly.
