# V68 — 幸せに必要な年収：学術資料と制作上の注意
人物、会議室、アパート、買い物、転職、日曜の会食は、研究結果を説明する架空の場面である。研究者の肖像・実際の研究室を再現するものではない。所得と幸福の多くは観察研究の関連であり、個人への因果効果を保証するものではない。

- Bernoulli, D. (1738). Specimen theoriae novae de mensura sortis. St Petersburg paradox and diminishing marginal utility of wealth. Historical thought experiment; not a modern happiness survey.
- Veblen, T. (1899). The Theory of the Leisure Class. Conspicuous consumption is a theoretical mechanism, not a claim about every consumer.
- Easterlin, R. A. (1974). Does Economic Growth Improve the Human Lot? Some Empirical Evidence. Debate on between-country and within-country income / well-being time series; not a universally accepted zero-growth finding.
- Kahneman, D. & Deaton, A. (2010). High income improves evaluation of life but not emotional well-being. PNAS 107, 16489–16493. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1011492107 — 2010-era US household income, retrospective daily emotions, ~USD 75,000 in that sample, not a Japanese personal salary cap.
- Killingsworth, M. A. (2021). Experienced well-being rises with income, even above $75,000 per year. PNAS 118, e2016976118. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2016976118 — 33,391 employed US participants, 1,725,994 experience-sampling reports.
- Killingsworth, M. A., Kahneman, D., & Mellers, B. (2023). Income and emotional well-being: A conflict resolved. PNAS 120, e2208661120. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2208661120 — adversarial collaboration, differential trends by baseline well-being; threshold described as a pattern in this sample, not universal.
- Whillans, A. V. et al. (2017). Buying time promotes happiness. PNAS 114, 8523–8527. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1706541114 — observational survey and a small field experiment; not proof that outsourcing is beneficial for everybody.
- 内閣府『満足度・生活の質に関する調査』各年度報告。家族・仕事・住居費・貯蓄・地域などの異質性に留意する。
