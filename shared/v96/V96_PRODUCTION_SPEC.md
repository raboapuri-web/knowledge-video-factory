# V96 Goosebumps — Production Specification

Title: 鳥肌ってなに？【進化生物学×自律神経×感情の神経科学】

Format: 1920×1080, 30fps, original Remotion vector/cinematic animation, VOICEVOX 青山龍星 speed 1.13, Japanese synchronized subtitles, subtle BGM.

Narrative structure: 26 distinct story/science worlds; 270–380 narration-aligned micro-scenes; minimum 140 continuity background groups. A background group may hold for no more than two adjacent micro-scenes and must never reappear after leaving it. Background geometry must stay frame-static inside a group, while foreground action changes according to narration.

Required worlds: midnight horror living room; winter station; concert hall build and peak; transparent skin cross-section; arrector pili contraction; autonomic laboratory; cat threat display; cold furry mammal; modern human arm/mirror; ancestral hot savanna; Darwin-era study; modern emotional piloerection lab; night street startle; music anticipation; music peak; PET/fMRI music brain; fear-vs-awe split scene; 2023 piloerection lab; skin-temperature visualization; hair-follicle microenvironment; cold-mouse research schematic; fever bedroom; thermoregulation control-room visualization; winter station return; private music listening; final skin/history montage.

Motion requirements: frequent cuts and small actions. Human pose library must include stand, sit, stand-up, seated-phone, walking-phone, walk, point, carry, reach, flinch, listen, shiver. Props must move or change state when narration meaning changes: drink raised/lowered, TV door opens, coat reacts to wind, orchestra bows move, hair rises, arrector muscle contracts, sympathetic signal travels, cat fur expands, papers turn, sensors blink, scanner ring moves, stimulus cards switch, thermal overlay changes, stem-cell nodes activate, thermometer rises, blanket pulled, train arrives.

Shot pattern rotates establish / medium / detail / reaction / overhead / push-in / cutaway / macro. No long static talking-head stretch. Same room must feel different across cuts through static but unique plate seeds, object layout, architecture, light direction and framing. Subtitles are the only readable overlay text.

Preflight must fail before VOICEVOX if: scene count outside 270–380, fewer than 140 backgrounds, nonconsecutive background reuse, any background used for >2 beats, phase count != 26, duplicate visual keys, TypeScript check failure, or critical still preview failure. Render only after preflight success. Release only after MP4 resolution/duration QA and contact-sheet generation succeed.
