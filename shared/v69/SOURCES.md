# V69 無料サービスと見えない代償：学術根拠と映像注意事項

主人公、家族、新聞売り、記者、放送局員、広告や課金の具体的な画面は、仕組みを説明するための架空の合成的表現であり、実在の個人や企業の実画面の再現ではない。サービスごとに収益モデルや個人情報の扱いは異なる。「無料」は常に搾取である、広告は常に有害である、行動データは常にアプリ間で共有される、などとは断言しない。

- 1833: Benjamin H. Day, The Sun, New York; penny press, low-price circulation and advertiser-funded media. Early paper was one penny, not a free newspaper.
- 1922-08-28: WEAF aired a paid advertising presentation for real estate. Radio was a mixed business model; do not imply the first ever commercial promotion globally.
- Herbert A. Simon (1971), Designing Organizations for an Information-Rich World. Attention scarcity as information supply expands; a conceptual insight, not a measured clinical finding.
- Rochet, J.-C., & Tirole, J. (2003), Platform Competition in Two-Sided Markets, Journal of the European Economic Association 1(4), 990–1029. https://doi.org/10.1162/154247603322493212 .
- Shampanier, K., Mazar, N., & Ariely, D. (2007), Zero as a Special Price: The True Value of Free Products. Marketing Science 26(6), 742–757. https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.1060.0254 . Study pertains to choices in particular experiments; not proof that all consumers always prefer free products.
- Klemperer, P. (1987), Markets with Consumer Switching Costs, Quarterly Journal of Economics 102(2), 375–394. https://doi.org/10.2307/1885068 .
- FTC (2024), A Look Behind the Screens: Examining the Data Practices of Social Media and Video Streaming Services. This is a regulatory investigation of particular companies, not evidence that every free app tracks across apps.
- Northwestern University, Medill Local News Initiative, State of Local News (2025). Newspaper closures reflect multiple economic and social factors, not just free online access.
- Network effects and switching costs are conditional economic mechanisms; avoid claims of inevitable monopoly.
- Opportunity cost of time: watching a video is not inherently a loss; compare the user's objective and alternatives.

Visual plan: fourteen individually built story locations, 210 narration-aligned micro-scenes, over 100 local continuity backgrounds, never reuse a scene plate after leaving it. A shared background stays perfectly static for two adjacent beats while animated foreground, actors, windows, physical props, screen states, advertisement cards, papers, instruments and objects evolve by narrative meaning. Subtitles are the only overlay text; no simulated real-world company branding or personally identifiable information.
