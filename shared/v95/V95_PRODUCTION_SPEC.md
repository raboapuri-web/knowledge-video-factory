# V95 Basic Income — Production Specification
Title: ベーシックインカムは世界を良くするのか？【労働経済学×福祉国家論×行動経済学】

1920×1080 / 30fps / VOICEVOX 青山龍星 speed 1.13 / Japanese synchronized subtitles / subtle BGM.
25 narrative worlds and 270–380 narration-aligned micro-scenes. Minimum 140 background continuity groups. A background group may be used for at most two adjacent micro-scenes and may never reappear later. Background layer must be frame-static inside a continuity group; all motion belongs to foreground actors, props, diagrams, vehicles, phone states, documents, carts, robots, cash, arrows and lighting overlays.

Required animated worlds: Tokyo bedroom morning; walking-with-phone street; commuter train sitting/standing; 1795 English farm and bread market; Spence/Paine map room; 1960s factory/office; household dining table; Dauphin town and farm kitchen; Alaska oilfield and snowy local economy; Finland winter apartment and job-search desk; U.S. cash-study household and spending scenes; Kenya red-dirt village/business/market; urban housing supply; government budget room; welfare-needs comparison room; AI warehouse; care facility/hospital/daycare; restaurant owner hiring; evidence-wall research room; final station.

Movement requirements: scene changes should be frequent and narration-specific. Use stand, sit, stand-up transition, seated-phone, walking-phone, walking, reading, pointing, carrying, serving, typing, opening envelope, moving papers, shopping, robot motion, market transaction, vehicle movement. Avoid static talking-head stretches. Within the same room, add/remove/move foreground objects as narration meaning changes. Shot kinds rotate establish/medium/detail/reaction/overhead/push-in/cutaway/macro without moving the held background plate.

Preflight must fail before VOICEVOX if scene count, phase count, visual uniqueness, background uniqueness, no-reuse rule, TypeScript/Remotion check, or critical still previews fail. Render only after preflight passes. Release MP4 only after 1920×1080 and duration QA and contact-sheet generation succeed.
