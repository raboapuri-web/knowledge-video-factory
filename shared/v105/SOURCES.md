# V105 research notes and production sources
The animated office worker, child, family and partner are **fictional examples** illustrating mechanisms; they are not real or representative case histories. Japanese narration and artwork were newly authored. Do not copy original narration, frames, score or illustrations from the inspiring third-party YouTube video.

1. Jeffrey Jensen Arnett (1994), *Are college students adults? Their conceptions of the transition to adulthood*, Journal of Adult Development 1, 213–224. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02277582 . The original study surveyed 346 US college students; 23% reported having reached adulthood. **Historical US undergraduate sample; do not treat it as a current Japanese adult population estimate.**
2. Shelly S. McCoy, Laura M. Dimler, Luiza Rodrigues, *Parenting in Overdrive: A Meta-analysis of Helicopter Parenting Across Multiple Indices of Emerging Adult Functioning*. Published online 18 September 2024; Journal of Adult Development (2025). https://doi.org/10.1007/s10804-024-09496-5 . Fifty-three correlational publications, 111 effect sizes; reported associations with academic adjustment, self-efficacy and regulatory skills. **Association does not prove parental behavior was the sole cause.**
3. Edward L. Deci and Richard M. Ryan (2000), *The “What” and “Why” of Goal Pursuits: Human Needs and the Self-Determination of Behavior*, Psychological Inquiry 11(4), 227–268. https://doi.org/10.1207/S15327965PLI1104_01 . Foundational framework for autonomy, competence and relatedness; theoretical construct, not a claim that a single experiment proved a causal effect.
4. 内閣府「令和5年版男女共同参画白書」第1節／特－8図. https://www.gender.go.jp/about_danjo/whitepaper/r05/zentai/html/honpen/b1_s00_01.html . In **2021**, for Japanese dual-earner married couples with children under six, wives accounted for 77.4% of couple household-related work time. Not a percentage for all couples or all years.
5. OECD, *Society at a Glance 2024*, published 20 June 2024. https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/society-at-a-glance-2024_918d8db3-en.html . Around half of **20–29-year-olds across OECD countries in 2022** lived with parents; marked country-specific variation. Not a Japanese-only statistic.
6. Y. Joel Wong et al., *Meta-analyses of the relationship between conformity to masculine norms and mental health-related outcomes*, Journal of Counseling Psychology (2017). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27869454/ . 78 samples, 19,453 participants; reported associations between some masculine norms and psychological help-seeking. These data do not imply every man or every masculinity-related trait is harmful.

Cinematic judgment in the story is editorial analysis, not a claim that these publications tested the exact fictional protagonists.
