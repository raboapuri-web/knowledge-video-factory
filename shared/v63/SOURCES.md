# V63 — 左遷と追放が残した作品：歴史・文学資料

The film distinguishes administrative demotion (左遷・貶謫), imperial relegation/exile (流刑・追放) and civic political banishment (亡命). Its composite scenic reenactments are not claims about historically documented speech, clothing, or exact rooms. Artistic readings are presented as interpretations, not causal proof that hardship improves creative ability.

## Interpretation guardrails

- Ovid was exiled, not merely demoted; Dante was a political exile, not a Chinese/Japanese-style court demotion.
- 『菅家後集』 is the exile-period collection; do not accidentally attribute the earlier 『菅家文草』 or the court poem 「秋思」 to composition in exile.
- Historical scenes of packing, nighttime writing, and individual gestures are explicitly dramatized visual reconstructions.
- 『前赤壁賦』 reflects a philosophical literary treatment, not an empirically proven psychological response caused by demotion.
- The closing argument (words survive a lost position) is a reading of this selected corpus; avoid a universal claim that punishment causes genius, and acknowledge survivorship bias.
