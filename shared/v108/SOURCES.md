# V108 Sources — なぜ人類は、同じ神々を何度も生み出すのか？

This file records source anchors for factual claims in the accepted narration. The film paraphrases rather than quotes.

- Kokugakuin University, Encyclopedia of Shinto / Kojiki-related reference pages: Yamata no Orochi, Izanagi and Izanami, Yomi traditions.
- Apollodorus, *Bibliotheca* 2 (via Theoi): Heracles and the Lernaean Hydra.
- Pausanias, *Description of Greece* 2 (via Theoi): ancient skepticism concerning the Hydra's multiple heads.
- *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* 10.16 (Vedabase): Krishna and Kaliya.
- Oxford Classical Dictionary / Oxford Research materials: Zeus, Dyaus Pitar, Jupiter and Indo-European etymological comparison.
- da Silva, Sara Graça & Tehrani, Jamshid J. (2016), "Comparative phylogenetic analyses uncover the ancient roots of Indo-European folktales", *Royal Society Open Science* 3:150645.
- LoBue, Vanessa & DeLoache, Judy S. (2008), "Detecting the Snake in the Grass", *Psychological Science* 19(3):284–289.
- Barrett-related minimally counterintuitive concept literature; 2006 *Cognitive Science* research on memorability and cultural transmission of minimally counterintuitive narratives.
- *Mahābhārata*, Vana Parva: Savitri and Satyavan.

Production note: Similarity does not itself establish direct historical transmission. The narration explicitly distinguishes common inheritance, diffusion, independent emergence, and cognitive selection.
