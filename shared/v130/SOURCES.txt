V130 Sources / 学術資料
Bernard Mandeville, The Fable of the Bees (1714). A satirical and philosophical fable, not a quantitative economic study.
John Maynard Keynes, The General Theory of Employment, Interest and Money (1936), especially the discussion of saving, investment, and effective demand.
Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, Wait, Is Saving Good or Bad? The Paradox of Thrift (2012).
Atif Mian and Amir Sufi (2010), Household Leverage and the Recession of 2007 to 2009.
Atif Mian and Amir Sufi (2012), What Explains High Unemployment? The Aggregate Demand Channel, NBER Working Paper 17830.
Atif Mian, Kamalesh Rao and Amir Sufi (2013), Household Balance Sheets, Consumption, and the Economic Slump.
McLeay, Radia and Thomas (2014), Money Creation in the Modern Economy, Bank of England Quarterly Bulletin.
Auerbach and Gorodnichenko (2012), Measuring the Output Responses to Fiscal Policy, American Economic Journal: Economic Policy.
Blanchard and Leigh (2013), Growth Forecast Errors and Fiscal Multipliers, IMF Working Paper 13/1.
