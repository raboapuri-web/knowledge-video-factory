# V65 Reference and interpretation
The video discusses work delegation conceptually, not a medical diagnosis or proof that one specific personality trait causes bad management. All persons and organizations are fictional.

* Hackman, J. R. (2002). Leading Teams: Setting the Stage for Great Performances. Harvard Business School Press. Team direction, enabling structures, supportive organizational context.
* Edmondson, A. (1999). Psychological Safety and Learning Behavior in Work Teams. Administrative Science Quarterly 44(2):350–383. https://doi.org/10.2307/2666999
* Deci, E. L. & Ryan, R. M. (2000). The "What" and "Why" of Goal Pursuits: Human Needs and the Self-Determination of Behavior. Psychological Inquiry 11(4):227–268. https://doi.org/10.1207/S15327965PLI1104_01
* Locke, E. A. & Latham, G. P. (2002). Building a Practically Useful Theory of Goal Setting and Task Motivation. American Psychologist 57(9):705–717. https://doi.org/10.1037/0003-066X.57.9.705
* Salancik, G. R. & Pfeffer, J. (1978). A Social Information Processing Approach to Job Attitudes and Task Design. Administrative Science Quarterly 23(2):224–253. https://doi.org/10.2307/2392563
* A task owner’s decision authority and a manager’s accountability are role-dependent and affected by law, contract, hierarchy, and organizational policy. This illustrative script does not claim a single fixed legal allocation.
* The examples are invented pedagogical situations, not accounts of documented employers.
