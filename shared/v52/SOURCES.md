# V52 Sources — Conscious Reality

Primary conceptual reference:
- Anil Seth, work on predictive perception, controlled hallucination, interoceptive inference, and the construction of self.
- Seth, A. K. (2013). Interoceptive inference, emotion, and the embodied self. Trends in Cognitive Sciences.
- Seth, A. K. (2021). Being You: A New Science of Consciousness.

Predictive processing / perception:
- Friston, K. (2010). The free-energy principle: a unified brain theory? Nature Reviews Neuroscience.
- Clark, A. (2013). Whatever next? Predictive brains, situated agents, and the future of cognitive science. Behavioral and Brain Sciences.

Rubber-hand illusion:
- Botvinick, M. & Cohen, J. (1998). Rubber hands 'feel' touch that eyes see. Nature 391, 756.

Color / illumination inference:
- Brainard, D. H. & Radonjic, A. (2014). Color constancy. The New Visual Neurosciences.
- Dress-perception research is used only as an illustration of ambiguous illumination inference; the narration avoids claiming a single universal mechanism for every observer.

Important caveat:
- Predictive processing is presented as an influential framework, not as a settled complete theory of consciousness. The script explicitly separates explanatory success in perception from the unresolved question of why neural activity is accompanied by subjective experience.
